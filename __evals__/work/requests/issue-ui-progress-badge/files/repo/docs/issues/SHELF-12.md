# SHELF-12 · Show reading progress on the shelf page

Readers can't tell how far along they are without opening the API.

## Done when
1. A book with status `reading` shows the share read in its badge, e.g. `Reading · 39%`.
2. Hovering or focusing that badge shows `page <pagesRead> of <pages>`.
3. `done` and `queued` badges are unchanged.
